SDR NIRMMAN - VIDEO ASSETS DIRECTORY
================================================================================

To add your 4 short-form property research videos/reels to the website:

1. Place your 4 MP4 video files inside this folder with the following filenames:
   - reel-1.mp4  (Video 1: e.g. Due Diligence / Property Title Research)
   - reel-2.mp4  (Video 2: e.g. GIS Coordinates & Boundary Survey)
   - reel-3.mp4  (Video 3: e.g. Registry Lineage & Encumbrance Verification)
   - reel-4.mp4  (Video 4: e.g. Field Inspection & Property Advisory)

2. (Optional) You can also place corresponding video poster thumbnail images:
   - poster-1.jpg
   - poster-2.jpg
   - poster-3.jpg
   - poster-4.jpg

The video player component on index.html is fully connected to these paths.
As soon as you add reel-1.mp4, reel-2.mp4, reel-3.mp4, and reel-4.mp4,
they will automatically play with touch/click controls on desktop and mobile!

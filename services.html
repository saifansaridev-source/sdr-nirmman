Core Deliverable

Property Research Report

A complete, audit-ready document tracing the history, registration legitimacy, and regulatory approvals of your target property.

This report compiles historical registry indexes, mutation certificates, layout approvals, and RERA compliance data. Our specialists pull documents directly from the sub-registrar files, leaving no room for hearsay or agent promises.

The Process: Intake property credentials → retrieve registry index copies → legal counsel review → generate audit report.
Key Benefit: Prevent buying double-allocated property or booking projects lacking active municipal clearances.
Order Report Audit
Legal Risk Intelligence

Property Risk Analysis

Identification of litigation history, hidden bank mortgages, inheritance claims, and pending regulatory notices.

Our legal research team queries state court databases, municipal tax databases, and national bank lien registries to discover if the target property has active disputes, unpaid tax bills, or attachments.

The Process: Deep query of district and High Court case files → banking mortgage registry audits → liability reporting.
Key Benefit: Ensure the seller holds unencumbered title deeds and has legal authority to sell.
Run Risk Analysis
Financial Diligence

Market Value Analysis

Scientific estimation of real property value based on registered transaction history, local indices, and development projections.

We check actual registered sales data in the local registry archives, analyze circle rate trends, evaluate regional infrastructure progress, and model the baseline value of the asset. This prevents premium overcharging by agents.

The Process: Extract comparative registry transaction values → analyze municipal circle rates → valuation report.
Key Benefit: Gain negotiation leverage with builders by knowing true historical transaction rates in the sector.
Order Valuation Audit
Spatial Auditing

Location Intelligence

Using geographic coordinates, satellite data overlays, and public zoning maps to secure boundaries.

We overlay land registry map sheets onto high-resolution satellite imagery and government master plans. We identify road overlaps, check easement rights, check forest zone setbacks, and calculate boundary coordinates.

The Process: Extract coordinate blueprints → overlay municipal master development charts → report overlaps.
Key Benefit: Stop boundary disputes before they happen and ensure no public zoning projects conflict with the land parcel.
Order Location Audit
Integrated Diligence

Due Diligence Coordination

Coordination of legal advisors, registry experts, and physical surveyors to compile unified reports.

Property due diligence requires coordination between multiple specialists. We serve as the primary coordinators, managing surveyors, querying registry offices, and consolidating the legal, geographic, and physical reports into one dashboard.

The Process: Set up multi-point checklist → assign surveyor & legal partners → consolidate research.
Key Benefit: Streamline the entire due diligence process under a single advisory point of contact.
Coordinate Diligence
Transaction Prep

Documentation Support

Drafting and reviewing purchase agreements, sale deeds, lease contracts, and power of attorney documents.

We review drafts of transaction documents to ensure protect-clauses, check payment schedules, verify legal definitions of boundaries, and confirm that all required registry formats comply with municipal land laws.

The Process: Document intake → clause check by legal counsel → redline reporting.
Key Benefit: Avoid hidden clauses, terms, or errors in deeds that could delay registry handovers.
Review Documents

Privacy Policy

Home Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 17, 2026

1. Scope of Privacy Safeguards

SDR Nirmman Private Limited ("SDR Nirmman", "we", "us") respects the privacy of our clients and site visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, verify, and protect property records, blueprint coordinates, contact information, and audit briefs shared during our property research processes.

2. Information Collection

In order to conduct our property research and due diligence audits, we collect both personal and property-specific information, including:

  • Client Identifiers: Name, phone numbers, corporate email addresses, and identification cards required for registry retrieval.
  • Property Documents: Title deeds, sale agreements, layout approvals, mutation records, survey charts, and coordinate blueprints.
  • Geographic Coordinates: Precise GPS coordinates or plot boundary charts of the target property.

3. Zero Developer/Broker Sharing Policy

IMPORTANT: We maintain a strict zero-sharing policy with third-party real estate brokers, developers, builders, and sales agents. All documents and blueprints submitted to SDR Nirmman are treated as strictly confidential legal work product.

Your property records will never be sold, leased, or distributed. We use your details solely to trace title histories and prepare the requested research reports.

4. Data Storage and Security

All property blueprints and legal documents are stored in secure, encrypted cloud repositories. Only the assigned legal counsel, registrar specialist, and surveyor are granted access to the files. Hard copies of reports are shredded upon completion of the consulting engagement unless explicitly requested otherwise by the client.

5. Amendments to this Policy

SDR Nirmman reserves the right to amend this Privacy Policy at any time in compliance with updated Indian cybersecurity and property regulations. Changes take effect immediately upon publication on this page.

6. Contact Privacy Desk

If you have any questions regarding data encryption or document security protocols, contact us at: privacy@sdrnirmman.com.

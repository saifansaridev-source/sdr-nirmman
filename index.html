Systematic Process

How We Conduct Property Research

Our research methodology follows a strict 6-stage workflow to guarantee the absolute precision of all findings.

1

Intake & Scope Definition

We retrieve details of the target property and define research requirements based on the location and development status.

2

Public Records Harvest

Our registry specialists pull documents from sub-registrar logs, court databases, municipality offices, and land survey departments.

3

Title & Ownership Tracing

Legal experts examine historical ownership lineage over 30 years to check for encumbrances, active disputes, bank liens, and inheritance issues.

4

GIS Mapping & Site Inspection

GIS coordinates are cross-verified with zoning records and physically cross-checked to highlight boundaries and prevent future encroachments.

5

Valuation & Micro-Market Analysis

Analysts model baseline market value using surrounding registered transactions data, local connectivity growth, and future master plans.

6

Report Handover & Consultation

A comprehensive research report is compiled and discussed over a private consulting session to support your final transaction decisions.
